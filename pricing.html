Party FAQ

Got Questions//, We Got You

Here's what people usually ask us before booking.

Can I book a party for fewer than 10 people?

Yes! Our rate is $8/hr per person. If you're staying longer, we recommend a Day Pass ($48/person).

Can I bring in food?

Yes, anything from pizza to cake. We have tables, plates and napkins available. The only restriction: outside snacks like chips, pop and energy drinks aren't allowed since we sell them here.

Can the party swap between PC and console?

Absolutely! Just let us know in advance so we can have everything set up when you arrive.

There are just a few of us. Do we have to book?

No need to book ahead. If you're a group of 5 or fewer it's easy to just walk in, sit down and play!
