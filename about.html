What Is a LAN?

Local Network//, 5ms Response

LAN stands for Local Area Network: nearly instant response times, as low as 5ms in some games, against people just a few feet away. Whether you're teaming up to take down that final raid boss or proving who's the better mid laner, there's no better feeling than playing with people you can see.
